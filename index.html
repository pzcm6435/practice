01 / Ethos

The Principle of Gravitas

“I craft volumes that endure beyond contemporary fleeting trends. Through material honesty and mathematical proportion, I transform raw matter into serene, meditative spatial experiences.”

Architecture should not shout; it must resonate through density, shade, and tectonic truth. My practice is an ongoing interrogation of permanence, extracting poetic light from massive structural anchors.

From seismic terrains in East Asia to quiet coastal towns, my work bridges local memory with progressive environmental engineering.

Every boundary is deliberate. Walls act as instruments calibrating temperature, natural breeze paths, and the acoustics of contemplative silence.

03 / Method & Scope

Four Disciplines, One Practice

I work as a single-track practice. Architecture, interior volume, custom millwork, and lighting physics are calibrated concurrently under strict material governance.

01 architecture

Architecture & Masterplanning

Site interventions, monolithic concrete framing, seismic engineering, and long-horizon structures designed to patina gracefully over decades.

02 chair

Interior & Bespoke Millwork

Seamless millwork carved from single solid logs, raw patinated bronze hardware, and monolithic stone vanities quarried per site.

03 wb_twilight

Spatial Light & Acoustics

Shadow choreography modeling diurnal solar paths. Micro-perforated acoustic treatments hidden beneath lime plaster to create temple-like silence.

04 science

Material Research

Proprietary pozzolanic cement mixes incorporating recycled volcanic slag, natural lime washes, and organic weathering tests over multi-year cycles.
